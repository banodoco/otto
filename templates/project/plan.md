## Outcome and scope

## Existing work and decision

## Sequence and readiness

Describe the shortest path, real dependencies, source or environment constraints, budget limits and unresolved decisions. Link task details to [tasks.md](tasks.md). Recording a plan does not authorize implementation.

## Interfaces and handoff

## Completion
